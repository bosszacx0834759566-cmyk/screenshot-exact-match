## Application conventions
- Keep the company experience in the single index route with section anchors; this preserves direct section links and continuous editorial navigation.
- Use the shared Button's editorial variant for unframed navigation and calls to action; this keeps interactions consistent without decorative boxes.
- Define editorial layout styles and monochrome roles in src/styles.css; this keeps responsive typography and theming centralized.
